# Design language audit, round 2, 2026-10-15 (#507)

This audit checks every screen built or reshaped since the first audit ([audit-2026-09-26.md](audit-2026-09-26.md)), against DESIGN.md's design language and the owner's brief. That covers Bills, a bill's page, Possible bills, Planned expenses (#202) and a plan's own page, Trends, Accounts, Settings' Suggestions and Tally's rules, Organize, Select mode, Add cash, the split form, Home's month history and Adjust mode, the error pages (404 and 500) and the first-visit states. Documents stays out (decision 66).

Each screen is judged the same way as the first audit: by **scenery**, **signage** and **use**, and by the owner's question: would someone who gave up on budgeting apps come back tomorrow?

- **Where the screens came from.** The local demo at `main` (bdbd5863, after #545), at 390×844 and 1280×800. CI's screenshots of the same pages are linked where CI takes them, for example [Bills, phone](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwilson21/tally/screenshots/pr-545/0130346/bills-phone.png) and [Trends, desktop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwilson21/tally/screenshots/pr-545/0130346/trends-desktop.png). CI doesn't take several of these screens yet (X2, below).
- **Family-app-only states** (Link a bank, a new family's first week) are still being built (#588 to #591). They're judged from their catalog specimens and P125 E's drawings when those PRs merge, not here.

Each finding names the layer, what's there now, what's missing or fighting, and where it goes. A finding that changes how something looks goes to the proposals page first, and the owner picks (decision 47). Nothing is fixed silently.
