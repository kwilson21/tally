# Design language audit, 2026-09-26 (#79)

Every current screen checked against DESIGN.md's design language: **scenery**, **signage** and **use**, and against the owner's brief. Would someone who gave up on budgeting apps come back tomorrow? And what would a careful designer have done that we skipped?

- **Tally's screens:** the CI screenshots at `main` (2fe1839), for example [Home, phone](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwilson21/tally/screenshots/pr-78/6ee9fbe/home-phone.png) and [Home, desktop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwilson21/tally/screenshots/pr-78/6ee9fbe/home-desktop.png).
- **The original's screenshots:** its catalog, run locally on its own fake data. They're in [original/](original/).

Each finding names the layer, what's there now, and what's missing or fighting. It also says where the original did better, and where the finding goes. **Decision** marks something only the owner can settle.

## Where the original isn't the bar

The original took care with states and guidance, and Tally should match that. Its visuals, though, are not the target:
- Its budget-setup flow says "0 recurring charges found" above five found bills, and files every bill under Entertainment ([original](original/flow-budget-setup-step1-390.png)).
- It uses emoji category icons, loud green and red cards, and a floating BETA button that covers content.

So Tally takes its **behavior** (guidance, states, feedback) and keeps its own **look**, restyled to the design language.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **The first screen on a phone (H1):** the one number should be on the first screen. Proposal: in the demo, move Things to try below the Budget list, and make the month title smaller than the number.
2. **Raw bank names (T1), until name suggestions (#33):** proposal: show them in sentence case with the card prefixes removed ("Doordash taco", "Cvs"), with the raw text still in the edit panel. That is code tidying a string, not AI deciding. Or leave them as they are until #33.
3. **MoneyInput (B1):** which original is your design, the catalog's stacked ±$1 box or the dialog's round buttons?
4. **Bars (H5):** thinner and lighter, so the words lead? Proposed as part of #86, shown in the catalog first.
